Anna Co-op · Our Process

Unlike any marketing firm in the world.

01

Anna-only, always.

We serve Anna, Texas businesses with Anna, Texas audiences — no exceptions, no out-of-town content, no diluted reach.

02

Connection is the mission.

We are as passionate about local businesses finding their community as we are about residents finding local solutions. This isn't advertising — it's infrastructure.

03

Sponsorships are tax-deductible.

Anna Arts Council is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Sponsorships support Anna's creative economy and are tax-deductible. Ad placements are standard business advertising purchases, not charitable contributions. Please confirm your own tax treatment with your advisor.

04

We teach — we don't do it for you.

We didn't build our platforms to profit from marketing. We built them to teach it. The Anna Co-op Academy partners with local businesses to understand their audience, navigate platform changes, track emerging trends, and grow their own capacity — not their dependence on an agency.

05

We diagnose before we prescribe — the REMEDY Report.

Before any recommendation, we run a REMEDY Report — a structured pain-point analysis across 30 industries and 150 documented business challenges. We identify what's actually costing you visibility, customers, and revenue. Then we prescribe only what fits your business, your audience, and your season. No generic packages. No guesswork.
